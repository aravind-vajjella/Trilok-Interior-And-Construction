What We Do

Complete Turnkey Solutions Under One Roof

From architectural blueprints and RCC foundations to the final layer of Italian polish — you deal with one single team with coordinated project planning.

House Plans & 3D Design

Vastu-conscious architectural layouts, municipal approval drawings, and photorealistic 3D virtual walkthroughs.

Civil Construction

Site excavation, foundation, RCC pillars, red brick masonry, plastering, and sump waterproofing by the civil engineering team.

Full Home Interiors

Complete 2BHK, 3BHK, and duplex interiors: TV units, living partitions, false ceilings, foyer consoles, and dining areas.

Modular Kitchens

Cabinetry, quartz worktops, pantry storage and soft-close hardware selected for your cooking routine and project budget.

Custom Wardrobes

Floor-to-ceiling sliding wardrobes, walk-in closets, lacquered glass, bronze profile shutters, and concealed organizers.

False Ceiling & Lighting

Gyproc Saint-Gobain ceilings with magnetic track lighting, 3000K warm cove LEDs, and architectural spot profiles.

Designer Bathrooms

Resort-style restrooms with large-format Italian tiles, backlit touch mirrors, and Grohe/Kohler concealed plumbing.

Sacred Pooja Mandir

Meet the Founder

The Vision Behind Trilok.

Mr. Sudipt Reddy Founder & CEO
"Every home we build carries a piece of our conviction — that good design and honest construction should go hand in hand."

With a civil engineering background and a deep passion for interior craftsmanship, Mr. Sudipt Reddy founded Trilok Interiors & Construction to bridge the gap between quality construction and thoughtful design in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

From the first foundation pour to the final finish, he personally oversees every project — ensuring material integrity, Vastu-conscious planning, and on-time delivery. His hands-on approach and transparent client relationships have made Trilok a trusted name across Hyderabad, Siddipet, Warangal, and beyond.
