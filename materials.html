Wardrobe Planning

Three Details for a Well-Planned Wardrobe

Combine full-height storage, bronze-tinted glass and well-positioned lighting. The bedroom concept shows how these details work together.

1. Bronze Tinted Toughened Glass

Semi-reflective bronze glass creates depth while subtly obscuring clothes, lighting up internally when doors slide.

2. Integrated Wardrobe Lighting

Lighting within shelves and hanging areas makes storage easier to use. Sensor controls can be specified with the chosen hardware.

3. A Place for Everyday Essentials

Plan a mix of hanging rails, shelves and drawers around the clothes and accessories you use. Internal fittings can be tailored to your routine.
